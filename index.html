Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

What corporate gifts does Blackthreads offer? +
Blackthreads offers a wide range of corporate gifts including branded T-shirts, polo shirts, jackets, caps, bottles, mugs, Bluetooth speakers, earphones, pen drives, power banks, umbrellas, gift hampers, smartwatches and fitness trackers. We also offer fully custom solutions for unique requirements.
What is the minimum order quantity? +
Minimum order quantities vary by product. Generally 50 pieces for apparel and 100 pieces for tech and drinkware items. We handle everything from small runs to 50,000-unit bulk orders. Contact us for specific requirements.
Does Blackthreads deliver across India? +
Yes, Blackthreads offers pan-India delivery from our offices in Trivandrum, Kerala and manufacturing unit in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. We ship reliably to any city, town or corporate campus across India.
What printing and branding techniques do you use? +
We use Screen Printing, HQ & 3D Embroidery, DTF Printing, Puff Printing, Laser Engraving, and 3D Printing. Each technique is chosen based on the product type and your branding requirements to ensure the best quality output.
How long does a corporate order take? +
Typical lead time is 7 to 15 working days depending on the product type and quantity. Rush orders are available on request. We provide regular status updates throughout the production process.
Can I see a sample before placing a bulk order? +
Yes, physical samples are available for large orders. We also provide digital artwork mockups for approval before any production begins. Contact us to discuss your sample requirements.
Which companies has Blackthreads worked with? +
Blackthreads has served 200+ leading companies including SBI, PNB, UST Global, Qburst, Royal Enfield, MRF, Allianz, Eureka Forbes, IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, KSEB, Terumo, Toonz, Envestnet, Speridian, and many more across India.
